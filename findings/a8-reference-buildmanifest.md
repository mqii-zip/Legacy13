# A8 Reference Found in iOS 13 Beta BuildManifest

## Finding
The iOS 13 beta BuildManifest for the iPhone 6s contains:

ApChipID = 0x8000

## Notes
0x8000 corresponds to the Apple A8 SoC used in:
- iPhone 6
- iPhone 6 Plus

## Possible Implications
- Legacy A8 support remnants may still exist
- Shared firmware components may still target A8 architecture
- Further kernelcache investigation required
